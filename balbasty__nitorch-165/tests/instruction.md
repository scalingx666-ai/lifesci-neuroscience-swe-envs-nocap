# Issue

**Title:** register: stop the Wolfe zoom early and drop the duplicate initial evaluation

## Description

## Summary
This PR implements two of the three decisions in #155: stop the strong Wolfe line search much earlier, and remove the duplicate initial evaluation (only where it has no side effects). It also fixes two affine-penalty bugs found along the way. The third item, reusing the line-search evaluation instead of `closure(param)`, stays open in #155, as decided.

Three commits:

1. **FIX: affine penalty crashed in optimiser runs; joint nonlinear steps dropped it.**
   - Optimisers pass `AffineModel.Parameters` to the step functions on their first evaluation, and `_affine_penalty` then failed on `lla.dot(logaff)`. On master, any registration with an affine penalty crashed. The #149 tests only passed tensors, and the CLI never sets an affine penalty.
   - `PairwiseRegisterStep.print` recorded `llv` but not `lla`. In a joint model, the nonlinear steps' objective therefore always used an affine penalty of 0, while the affine steps' objective included it, and the interleaved convergence test compared the two.
2. **PERF: stop the strong Wolfe zoom once the loss is flat.**
   - Near convergence, the zoom kept bracketing until the bracket was smaller than 1e-9 or it reached 25 trials, each with a gradient evaluation, for gains below float32 resolution.
   - New `ftol` (default 1e-6, relative): once a bracket is found, the search stops as soon as a trial is within `ftol · max(|f_best|, |f0|)` of the best point, and keeps that point.
   - The test is two-sided, so a clearly worse trial (normal backtracking after an overshoot) does not stop the search. It does not apply during the expansion phase, where the loss changes slowly because the step grows slowly, not because it has converged.
   - There is deliberately no hard cap on the number of trials. On the profiled cases, the productive part of long searches was the expansion (up to 12 trials); the wasted part was the flat tail.
   - `ftol=0` restores the previous behaviour.
3. **PERF: do not evaluate the data term twice at the initial parameters.**
   - `PairwiseRegister.fit` evaluated the loss before handing over to the optimiser, which evaluates it again, with derivatives, at the same parameters. That was one duplicate evaluation per optimiser run (two in joint mode): the `001 | … | gain 0` row.
   - The evaluation is kept unchanged for stateful losses (`get_state()` is not None: EMMI, GMM, MAD/Tukey), since it initialises their warm-start/IRLS state.
   - Otherwise, only the penalty terms that the two steps of a joint model take from each other (`llv`, `lla`) are initialised.
