# Issue

**Title:** svf: weight the exp_backward push forward by |det J| (fixes indefinite Hessian where the field folds)

## Description

## Summary
This fixes a regression from #245, found while profiling the FMG solver on real data (#158).

**The bug.** `exp_backward` pushes the gradient and Hessian forward through each squaring step, and weights them by the Jacobian determinant of the step: `hess *= det`, `grad *= det`.
- Before #245, the step Jacobian was obtained by composing near-identity Jacobians, so its determinant stayed positive.
- #245 computes it by finite differences of the squared field. That gives the true determinant, which is **negative where the field folds**. The weighted Hessian then has negative diagonal entries there.

**Effect on a real inter-subject SVF**: MSC02 → MSC01 T1w, MSE, 0.8 mm, 224×256×256.
- At the finest level, 49 voxels had a negative Hessian diagonal (down to −7.2). The field folds in 0.1% of voxels.
- The Gauss-Newton CG/FMG solve on this indefinite system returned NaN everywhere. `GridCG.search_direction` replaces non-finite values with 0, so the step was zero, the line search accepted `a = 1` with no change, and the level stopped after one iteration with gain 0.
- In other words, the finest pyramid level made no progress at all.

**The fix.** The push forward is a change of variables, whose weight is the volume change `|det J|`. Weighting by `abs(det)` keeps the Hessian positive semi-definite, and changes nothing where the field does not fold.
