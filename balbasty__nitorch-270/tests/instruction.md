# Issue

**Title:** register: early stopping on the size of an update (@@optim -d/--disp-tolerance)

## Description

## Summary
This implements the displacement-based criterion of #266 with **conservative defaults**, so that it can go in now. The loss criterion is unchanged, and so is its default. Making `-c gain` the default and tuning both thresholds come later, in #266.

**Rule.** A pyramid level now stops as soon as **either** of these holds, or at `max_iter`:
- the loss tolerance is met (`-t`/`-c`, unchanged);
- the last accepted update is smaller than `@@optim -d/--disp-tolerance`.

`-d 0` disables the new criterion.

**Size of an update.** It is the RMS of the displacement the update causes, over the (masked) voxels of the images the losses are evaluated on. Each displacement is measured in its own image's voxel coordinates, at the current pyramid level.
- **Images:** each loss's `fixed` image, warped by the forward transform. For the reversed half of a symmetric loss, that is the original moving image, warped by the inverse transform, so symmetric losses count both images.
- **Affine:** exact, in closed form per image: `tr(R E[x̃ x̃ᵀ] Rᵀ)` with `R = F₃⁻¹ (A₁ − A₀)`, where `F` is the image's voxel-to-world matrix and `A⁻¹` is used for backward terms. RAS displacements are therefore converted to voxels axis by axis, so anisotropic voxels are handled. The second moments are computed once per level.
- **smalldef:** exact, the RMS of the displacement update.
- **svf, shoot:** the RMS of the velocity update `Δv`. That is the first-order estimate of the displacement change: `exp(v+Δv) − exp(v) ≈ Δv`, transported and stretched by the flow, and `−Δv` for the inverse.
  - Lattice voxels are mapped to image voxels (`F₃⁻¹ L₃`), so a coarser velocity lattice counts correctly (e.g. `@nonlin -v 2 mm`).
  - Each lattice voxel is weighted by the number of image voxels it covers, so images at different resolutions combine correctly.

**Defaults:** `1e-3` voxel for affine and `1e-2` voxel for nonlinear (`AFFINE_DISP_TOLERANCE`, `NONLIN_DISP_TOLERANCE` in `pairwise_makeobj`). They only stop runs whose updates have become negligible.

**Cost:** one copy of the parameters per iteration while the criterion is enabled (one field for nonlinear models), plus one weighted pass over each image per level.

## Implementation
- `OptimIterator(disp_tol=0, step_size=None)`: generic. `step_size(prev, new)` is a hook, and the iterator records `last_step_size`.
- `PairwiseRegister._set_step_sizes()` attaches the hooks: `_affine_step_size` and `_nonlin_step_size`, on the targets from `_step_targets(losses)`.
- `make_affine_optim` and `make_nonlin_optim` take `disp_tolerance`.
- CLI: `@@optim -d/--disp-tolerance`.

## On real data (with the defaults)
Midnight Scan Club (ds000224), 0.8 mm, 224×256×256, C backend:
- **Rigid MSE, T1w run-02 → run-01:** the loss criterion stops first at every level. The run is unchanged.
- **smalldef MSE, MSC02 → MSC01 (affinely pre-aligned):** RMS of each accepted update, in voxels, with the default 10 iterations per level:

  | level | iterations 1 → 10 |
  |---|---|
  | 2 (coarsest) | 0.95 0.65 0.54 0.52 0.51 0.50 0.23 0.14 0.09 0.08 |
  | 1 | 0.51 0.39 0.39 0.40 0.19 0.10 0.07 0.06 0.05 0.05 |
  | 0 (finest) | 0.45 0.30 0.15 0.08 0.05 0.04 0.04 0.04 0.04 0.04 |

  - At the finest level, the update falls to about 0.04 voxel by iteration 5–6, then creeps at 0.037 voxel per iteration.
  - The `1e-2` default never fires within 10 iterations, so the run is unchanged.
  - A nonlinear default around `0.05` would stop the finest level after about 5 iterations instead of 10, halving its cost. Its accuracy cost is still to be measured (#266).
